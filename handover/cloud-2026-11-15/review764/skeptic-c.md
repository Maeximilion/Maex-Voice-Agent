### [P2] Outage path reads the team number before the outage sentence plays
Verdict: CONFIRMED (defect P2; effective P1 under docs/21_REVIEW_AUTOMATION.md:136, code on the fate of a call / outage fallback)
Evidence: probes-c/test_probe_c_order.py re-run red in rev-h through the production composition (`CallSession` + `Lines` + real `CallHandler`, as built in api/telephony/app.py:317,432-433): actions `answer, play(greeting), team_number_read, play(SAY_OUTAGE)`. Mechanism holds: `_fail` (handler.py:666-673) calls `self._port.say` then `_team_phone`, but inside a session turn `Lines.say` only appends to `_asked` (session.py:277-283) and is carried out after `deliver()` returns (session.py:664-678). So the comment at handler.py:671-672 and docs/20_VOICE_LAYER.md:227 ("after the outage sentence has gone out") are false on the voice layer; they hold only on an adapter that speaks synchronously (FakeTelephony). No test pins the order; nothing else in the path reorders it.
Timing against the documented budget: the only stated limit for this row is 30 s from the cut or crash to the team ringing (docs/20:371 "within the limit of 30 s"; docs/20:730, the go of T-10.2). Measured before #754: 12.4 s (docs/20:371). The extra silence is not measured; the reviewer's "about 4 s" is a derivation. My reading: the turn's failed statement is a disconnect, so SQLAlchemy invalidates the pool and `begin_nested` makes a fresh connect (connect_timeout 2 s, api/db.py:66-73) rather than a pre-ping plus a connect; but the name lookup of `db` before libpq's connect is not bounded by connect_timeout, and docs/20:255,258,372 show lookups of several seconds on the workbench (8.3 s to the team for a cut before a call). So the added silence is roughly 0 to 6 s, giving about 12 to 19 s: it does not break the 30 s limit. There is no stated mid-call silence limit it breaks (turn_deadline 10 s at docs/20:125 is the core's turn, outside `_fail`; the 10 s at docs/20:220 is before answer). The doc's "bound by the line's 2 s statement limit" is also inexact: a fresh connect is bounded by connect_timeout plus an unbounded lookup, not by statement_timeout. The model-down row of the 14.11 wire run (after #754, docs/20:934, "4 s after the failure") had a healthy database and does not exercise this.
Fix: in `_fail`, read the number only once the queued outage sentence is out, e.g. read it before the rollback only when the database is not the failure, or, simpler, keep `live.team_phone` on the `_fail` path when the failure was a database error (`sqlalchemy.exc.DBAPIError`/`OperationalError`) and re-read only otherwise; or let `Lines` resolve the transfer target lazily after the sentence. At minimum correct the comment and docs/20:227. · Test: the reviewer's probe (own voice layer, `current_team_phone` instrumented, assert `play(SAY_OUTAGE)` before the read), plus one where the read raises after a DB error and the call's own number is used without a second read; re-run the "cut from the database mid-call" drill and record the new time in docs/20 §5. · Needs Maxi: no (plain fix; the drill re-run is Claude's on the workbench).
