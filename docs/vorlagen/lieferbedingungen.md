# Lieferbedingungen (Entwurf)

> Draft, not legal advice (limits of D20). Serves N2 of the review of 02.11.2026 (`docs/09_OPERATIONS_LEGAL.md` section 1): a delivery by phone is a distance contract, there is no right of withdrawal for freshly prepared food (§ 312g(2) no. 2 BGB, goods that spoil quickly; no. 9 as the fallback, check of 11.11.2026), the information duties stay (§ 312d BGB, Art. 246a EGBGB). Maxi fills in the seller identity and publishes the page on the website. **A printed copy goes with every delivery, and with every pickup ordered by phone, together with the register's receipt:** § 312f(2) BGB asks for a confirmation of the contract on a durable medium at the latest at delivery, and the receipt alone does not carry the notice on withdrawal. The delivery fee and the minimum order are not written here: they come from the database (`delivery_zones`), so the page points to the amount the caller hears.

### Kein Widerrufsrecht

Unsere Speisen werden frisch für Sie zubereitet und verderben schnell. Für ihre Lieferung besteht deshalb kein Widerrufsrecht (§ 312g Abs. 2 Nr. 2 BGB; soweit die Lieferung zu einem vereinbarten Zeitpunkt als Dienstleistung gilt, auch § 312g Abs. 2 Nr. 9 BGB).
